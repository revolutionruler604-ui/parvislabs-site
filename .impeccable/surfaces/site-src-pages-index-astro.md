OWN-WORLD: Pale plaster ground, bronze-black ink, weathered pine grey for secondary text, iris blue as the only saturated colour and only on things that move or can be pressed. One continuous drawn line with round joints; spheres; a bronze bowl. A flared Japanese mincho for display in sentence case, a plain gothic for text. No boxes around content, no rules, no cards.
