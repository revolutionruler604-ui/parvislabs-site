## Platform

web

## Users

Confirmed Nov 13 2026.

- First reader: anyone curious. Someone who gets the link cold, on a phone or between meetings, gives it a few seconds, and might pass it on.
- Hiring managers and founders arrive the same way. They find the depth one click in: how the work is run, and what each project does.
- Job of the visit: understand in seconds that one person directs a crew of AI agents and ships real products, enjoy it, then write in.

## Product Purpose

One link for Parvis Labs. It advertises the studio: what has been built, how it gets built, and what comes next. Success is an email to the Parvis Labs hello address. The second measure is whether a visitor sends the link to someone else.

The site is a frame that keeps growing. Each new project that works gets added to it.

## Positioning

One founder with a day job, and a crew of AI agents that plan, build and review each other's work. The founder's time is the scarce input and the record shows how little of it a change takes. A neighboring portfolio can list projects. It cannot show a working crew, its rules, and the products that crew shipped.

## Operating Context

- Projects shown: Rich Life (a money app for couples), Blackfin (a trading bot that works alone inside limits it cannot change), Frame Board (a home and family planning system that lives on the kitchen TV, with the phone as the remote).
- In the lab: Language Coach (a speaking tutor), Atlas (an analysis engine for hotel revenue management).
- The method: a change goes from plan to build to review to fix to decision to record, each step with one owner. The record is a log of every change.
- The Blackfin section names its real schedule in New York time: it wakes at 10:32 and 3:45 each trading day.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site built with Astro. Fonts and scripts are served from the site itself. No tracking across sites.
- Hosting is moving to Cloudflare (Workers with static assets). A private preview comes first. No DNS change without the founder's yes.
- Every published word gets a review by a model that did not write it before it reaches the live site. The founder approves a huge change: a ground-up rework, a new page or project, a change to what a project may show, or anything that touches DNS or hosting.
- The chicken coop project is removed from the site (Nov 13 2026).
- Rich Life: only the invented showcase household may appear. Never real household figures.
- Blackfin: method, architecture and the investment thesis, plus counts of stocks read, orders refused and trades made, and one results figure: the account's percent change since it went live, shown live on the home page. Never a dollar amount, the account's value, the starting amount, tickers, share counts or account details.
- Frame Board: a real board rendered from randomized, invented household data. No home network, doorbell or account details.
- Atlas: a short description, plus one small demo on an invented hotel, captioned as made up. No employer name, data, property names, rates or any money figure, or internal terms.
- No named competitor on the page. Say what most money apps do and show what Rich Life does instead.
- Light and dark, phone first.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Parvis Labs. One line carries the only personal name: "Founder Sachin Kumar". No photo of the founder.
- Voice: "we", with the agents as the team. Plain US English, short sentences, no em dashes, no builder jargon. Technical but approachable. Humor is wanted. So is a little human bafflement at how odd this way of working is.
- Show, never tell. A quality is proved with a fact, a working thing or a design choice.
- The site may take risks. It is an advertisement and an exhibition of what can happen next, not only a record of what happened.
- Of the first three art directions, the founder preferred the light paper one, barely. The typefaces of that round are rejected.
- Reference the founder named for spirit, not for look: a personal site that is one sentence and one toy.

## Evidence on Hand

- Rich Life: the running app on its invented showcase household, including the money-flow chart, the three views of one household, and the shared plan.
- Blackfin: its written proposal, the guard that checks every order, its journal design and its schedule. Its thesis is small-company drift after earnings, long only.
- Frame Board: the real board renderer, run on invented data.
- The method: a log of changes with builder, reviewer, fix rounds, serious findings and founder minutes. Figures on the site are computed from the rows and carry their date.
- Absent, and not to be invented: customers, testimonials, press, any trading result beyond the one percent figure, revenue, user counts.

## Product Principles

1. The first screen is something to do, not something to read.
2. Facts stay true and dated. Ambition is stated as what comes next.
3. Every project earns its place with one thing a visitor can see working.
4. The frame outlives its contents: adding a project is a small change.
5. One action: write to the hello address.
