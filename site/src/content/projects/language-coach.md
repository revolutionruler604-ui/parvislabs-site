Language Coach is meant to be a voice-first tutor for one learner and one score: how well you speak. The planned session runs fifteen to twenty minutes. You talk with a tutor that stays in the language and does not interrupt. Then you tell the same story again, shorter and faster. At the end come up to three corrections, and you say each fixed sentence aloud. They return later as spoken review. Its one measure of effort is how many minutes you speak. There are no streaks and no points. When the session ends, it says "Done for today."

**Where it stands:** A spoken conversation on the phone works today. The first real one ran in Mandarin on November 12, 2026. The retell and the corrections come next, then spoken review.
